Commands

Everything the MIHU bot can do. Commands marked [STAFF] can only be run by staff — every other command is available to all members.

Verification

6 commands
CommandAccessDescriptionExamples
/verify Everyone Verifies yourself: sets your nickname to 🌸 and stores your rank. Ranks: Celestial, Immortal, Emperor, Shogun, Samurai, Sensei, Elder, Ronin, Shinobi, Guardian, Warrior, Ranger, Nomad, Wanderer.
/verify Mihaitzuuu Celestial .verify Mihaitzuuu Celestial
/rank update Everyone Sends a rank-change request to the staff channel.
/rank update Samurai
/rank change @member Staff Change a member’s rank (also assigns the matching Discord role if one exists).
/rank change @Elite Celestial
/unverify @member Staff Completely removes a member from the bot records.
/unverify @User
/trust @member Staff Marks a member as trusted at a location (DMs the member). Locations: mihu-farm, mihu-rentals, mihu-shop, mihu-casino, mihu-money, dungeon.
/trust @Elite mihu-farm
/untrust @member Staff Removes trust at a location (DMs the member). Same locations.
/untrust @Elite mihu-farm

Prefix equivalents

Slash ↔ Prefix

Both slash commands (/command) and prefix commands (.command) work and are equivalent. Here’s how they map:

SlashPrefix
/verify Mihaitzuuu Celestial .verify Mihaitzuuu Celestial
/points view .points view
/warn add @Elite reason .warn @Elite reason
/cmd add website .cmd add website
— .website triggers a custom tag
🌸 Legend: Staff  = staff-only · Everyone  = available to all members. Angles mean a required argument; brackets [optional] mean optional.
